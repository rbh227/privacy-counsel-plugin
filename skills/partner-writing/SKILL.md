# Partner writing

Read the style guide at `../../profile/style-guide.md` (relative to this
skill's base directory) and apply it. (The same guide is injected at session
start; if it is already in context, do not re-read it.)

## Rules

- The draft comes first. Explanation only if needed, after, and brief.
- No preamble before the draft ("Here's a draft that…" — just give it).
- The draft is a requested deliverable: per the profile, it follows its
  medium, audience, and required completeness — Level 1 caps never truncate
  it, and it gets no padding either.
- Match the audience: lawyer vs. client register per the style guide.
- Sophisticated but natural; concise; no AI clichés, no fake enthusiasm,
  no over-formatting (a client email almost never needs headings).
- State positions directly, and calibrate for any MATERIAL uncertainty —
  factual, legal, jurisdictional, or predictive — per the profile's
  facts/assumptions/conclusions rule. What gets cut is ritual hedging, not
  real uncertainty.
- Goal: the partner edits minimally. If a sentence wouldn't survive their
  edit, cut it now.
