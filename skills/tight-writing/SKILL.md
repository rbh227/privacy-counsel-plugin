# Tight writing (on request)

Rewrite the given text to be as short as it can be without losing substance.

Cut:
- redundant qualifiers and double hedges
- repeated arguments and restated conclusions
- generic transitions and throat-clearing openers
- headings that outnumber their content
- explanations of things a senior lawyer knows
- anything written to sound thorough rather than to inform

Rules:
- Return the tightened text first, no commentary. If a legally meaningful
  distinction had to survive at the cost of length, it survives — brevity
  loses to legal nuance.
- Preserve the author's voice (or the style guide's, if it was our draft).
- If asked "what did you cut", answer in 2–3 bullets.
